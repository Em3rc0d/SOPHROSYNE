# Quarries

Quarries are unresolved, evidence-seeking workstreams. They prevent important assumptions from disappearing into implementation.

## Active MK0 quarries

### Q01 — Peru Regulatory Boundary
**Status:** OPEN / `FATAL_IF_FAILED`

Question: which exact SOPHROSYNE behaviors remain general research/decision-support, and which cross into regulated personalized advice, recommendation, intermediation or execution?

Closure receipt: written review from qualified Peruvian securities counsel covering actual onboarding, portfolio context, scenario cards, alerts, strategy sandbox, pricing/marketing and future connector flows.

### Q02 — Commercial Data Rights
**Status:** OPEN / `FATAL_IF_FAILED`

Question: can every planned source be used commercially under our exact display/non-display/derived-data/retention architecture at sustainable cost?

Closure receipt: provider-by-provider rights matrix with written terms, price, attribution, entitlement requirements and fallback source.

### Q03 — Willingness to Pay
**Status:** OPEN

Question: will target users repeatedly use and pay for evidence-based market translation/verification instead of raw charting or generic AI?

Closure receipt: behavioral interviews, chart-vs-translator test, pricing results, retention proxy and paid-pilot evidence.

### Q04 — Quant Baseline & ML Increment
**Status:** OPEN

Question: what incremental value, if any, do regime/multimodal/ML models add over deterministic baselines after fees, slippage, leakage controls and out-of-sample validation?

Closure receipt: reproducible experiment manifest and benchmark-ladder results.

### Q05 — Moat & Competitive Durability
**Status:** OPEN

Question: do evidence graph, Decision Ledger, uncertainty/risk-first UX and beginner-to-quant progressive disclosure create durable preference/retention beyond language localization?

Closure receipt: prototype preference tests, repeat usage, trust metrics and competitive refresh.

## Closure rule

A quarry closes only with an attached receipt: official source, contract, legal opinion, reproducible experiment or direct observed user evidence. Implementation alone never closes a quarry.
