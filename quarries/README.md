# Quarries

Quarries are unresolved, evidence-seeking workstreams. They prevent important assumptions from disappearing into implementation.

## Active MK0 quarries

### Q00 — Core Causal Value
**Status:** OPEN

Question: does the full candidate SOPHROSYNE intervention create material incremental decision-process value beyond competent simple and cheap/free substitutes, or is most value explained by friction, structure, simple feedback or short memory?

Execution authority: `experiments/q00/Q00_PREREGISTRATION.md`.

Closure receipt: preregistered comparative/ablation experiment with frozen baselines, hidden scoring, transfer/cognitive-cost analysis, adverse evidence and independent review.

Q03 willingness-to-pay, Q04 modeling sophistication and Q05 moat evidence cannot rescue a failed Q00 configuration.

## Closure rule

A quarry closes only with an attached immutable evidence receipt: official source, contract, legal opinion, reproducible experiment or direct observed user evidence, according to the authority defined by `docs/validation/EVIDENCE_CLOSURE_PROTOCOL.md`.

Implementation alone never closes a quarry. `INCONCLUSIVE` never auto-promotes to PASS. No downstream success may compensate for a failed fatal or causal gate.
