## Source policy

Promoted claims should be tagged where appropriate as `OFFICIAL`, `ACADEMIC`, `OBSERVED`, `INFERRED` or `HYPOTHESIS`.

Vendor marketing claims must never be promoted as independent facts without corroboration or explicit labeling.

A source URL alone is not a receipt. A receipt should record the exact claim used, access date, provenance class, relevance to a lock/quarry, and whether the evidence remains current.
