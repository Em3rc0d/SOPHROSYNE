# Research Participant Protocol

## Purpose

This governs human research used by Q03/Q05. It is product research, not investment activity and not a substitute for any formal ethics/legal review that may apply in a later context.

## Eligibility / geography

- promotion studies recruit adults `18+`;
- current MK1 primary promotion geography is `Peru` and primary study language is `Spanish`;
- other cohorts are explicitly exploratory unless separately preregistered for promotion;
- recruitment source and selection bias are logged.

Do not recruit minors for investment-product validation.

## Consent

Before data collection, participants receive a versioned plain-language notice covering purpose, tasks/duration, data/telemetry/recordings, compensation, voluntary participation/withdrawal, retention/deletion process, and the fact that the study is product research—not investment advice—and requires no real-money trade.

## Prohibited practices

Studies must not require/encourage live trades, condition compensation on investment behavior/performance, request brokerage/exchange passwords/API secrets/seed phrases/full account numbers, fabricate urgency/returns/regulatory approval, hide a non-charging fake door, or collect sensitive financial data without a documented need.

## Compensation and contamination control

Compensation rules are frozen before recruitment. Compensation never depends on favorable feedback, WTP, purchase intent, simulated return, use frequency or unprompted returning.

For E03-C/Q05 longitudinal behavioral evidence, compensation may cover participation/onboarding or a fixed disclosed study obligation, but it must not reward the very organic return behavior used as a primary metric. Any mandatory scheduled check-in is labeled prompted/research-required and excluded from organic-return numerators.

## Contact/reminder policy

Reminder cadence, channels and attribution windows are frozen before the longitudinal study. Over-prompting that would manufacture return behavior is prohibited. Sessions near a reminder are conservatively classified as prompted/ambiguous under the frozen rule.

## Data minimization / identity separation

Prefer age eligibility over birth date, experience bands over detailed finances, coarse portfolio categories over exact holdings, and pseudonymous participant IDs. Contact identity, if retained, is stored separately and more restrictively than research telemetry.

## Scenario rule

Controlled tests use synthetic or true point-in-time historical scenarios with equivalent underlying evidence across variants unless evidence difference itself is the preregistered treatment. Participants are not told what real asset to buy/sell.

## Moderator / recording

Moderator scripts are versioned; leading prompts are prohibited and deviations are logged. Audio/video/screen recording is opt-in only when needed. Retention and deletion behavior is frozen before recruitment.

## Telemetry privacy

Never log secrets, raw auth headers, full account identifiers, payment-card data, unrelated free text, or exact portfolio positions without explicit approved necessity.

## Withdrawal / deletion

The manifest freezes the identifiable deletion window and treatment of already anonymized/aggregated outputs. Removal is recorded transparently and denominators are recomputed under the frozen rule; no silent denominator editing.

## Adverse participant signals

Pause/review if participants interpret the prototype as individualized instruction despite framing, feel pressure to transact, disclose credentials because the UI implies they are required, cannot distinguish experimental/production status, or are confused about broker/adviser identity. These observations are evidence for Q01/Q03.

## Completion checklist

Consent/version, eligibility, geography/language, recruitment source, compensation policy, contact/reminder policy, exclusions, moderator deviations, pseudonymous telemetry, missing/withdrawn data, raw-artifact ownership/retention and prohibited-data checks must all be documented before a participant-based receipt is final.
