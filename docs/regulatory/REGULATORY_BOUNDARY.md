# Regulatory Boundary

> Product-risk map only. Not legal advice.

## MK1 regulatory posture

SOPHROSYNE should initially behave as non-custodial research/decision-support software.

Candidate low-risk product behaviors for legal review:
- generalized market explanation;
- descriptive portfolio analytics;
- scenario analysis;
- non-personalized research;
- user-authored rules;
- historical testing;
- paper testing;
- `rule triggered / not triggered`.

Do not ship until counsel specifically approves:
- personalized “you should buy/sell X”;
- ranking securities based on a user's financial circumstances;
- discretionary/autonomous execution;
- copy trading;
- custody;
- strategy marketplace with implied recommendations.

## Peru closure requirement

Obtain a written securities-law review of the exact:
1. onboarding;
2. portfolio context flow;
3. market/scenario cards;
4. alerts;
5. strategy sandbox;
6. pricing page;
7. marketing copy;
8. disclaimers/terms;
9. future broker connector design.

## International expansion

Do not assume “software” avoids financial regulation. Personalization, compensation, discretion, execution and distribution all matter. EU/US expansion requires its own legal gate.

## Copy policy

Avoid:
- AI financial adviser;
- predicts the market;
- beats the market;
- safe profit;
- guaranteed signal.

Prefer:
- market intelligence;
- decision-support;
- scenario analysis;
- evidence and risk;
- user-defined strategy testing.
