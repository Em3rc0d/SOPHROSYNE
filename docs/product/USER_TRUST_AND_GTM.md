# Users, Trust & Go-to-Market

## Primary persona

**The capable beginner**: technically comfortable, willing to invest, but unwilling to spend months becoming a chartist before making sense of markets.

Needs:
- lower time-to-understanding;
- less information overload;
- explicit uncertainty and risk;
- explanations that preserve nuance;
- a way to test rules before trusting them.

Anti-needs:
- more indicators for their own sake;
- opaque confidence scores;
- constant pressure to trade;
- guru language.

## Secondary persona

**The systematic retail trader**: already understands indicators but wants reproducible workflows, backtest discipline, regime context and an audit trail.

## Future persona

**Developer / fintech integrator**: consumes structured market-state, evidence and validation APIs. Not an MK1 GTM priority.

## Trust model

SOPHROSYNE must structurally reject:
- guaranteed-return claims;
- cherry-picked screenshots;
- hidden backtest windows;
- unexplained confidence;
- retroactively edited signals;
- deleting losing/failed records;
- fabricated urgency designed to increase trading frequency.

Required behavior:
- show supporting and opposing evidence;
- expose stale/missing data;
- expose model/ruleset version;
- expose uncertainty and invalidators;
- preserve failed analyses;
- allow `NO CONCLUSION` and `NO TRADE`.

## Positioning

Lead with:
- understand before acting;
- evidence over impulse;
- risk before excitement;
- progressive learning;
- reproducible reasoning.

Do not compete on “more indicators” or “more signals”.

## Candidate channels

- educational content demonstrating real market interpretation;
- SEO around “what happened / what does this mean” questions;
- communities of developers, young professionals and self-directed investors;
- public evidence/decision-history artifacts that demonstrate trust;
- broker/fintech partnerships later.

## B2C → B2B option

If consumers value the engine but subscription economics fail, test an embeddable/API product for brokers and fintechs. This is a separate validation track, not permission to skip B2C evidence.
