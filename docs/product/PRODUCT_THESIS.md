# Product Thesis

## Problem

Retail investors can access large volumes of financial information but often lack the mental models needed to turn it into disciplined, risk-aware decisions.

The opportunity is not “more indicators”. It is **translation + evidence + uncertainty + discipline**.

## Primary JTBD

> Help me understand what is happening, what evidence supports each plausible scenario, what could invalidate it, and what risk I am taking before I act.

## Product category

**Financial Decision Intelligence**

Not “AI trading guru”, not “autonomous hedge fund”, not “signal Telegram”.

## Core promise

> Transform market uncertainty into understandable evidence, scenarios, risk and disciplined decisions — without pretending certainty.

## Product modes

### EXPLAIN
Translate market state and evidence into plain language.

### STRATEGIZE
Turn user-authored ideas into explicit, testable rules.

### VERIFY
Backtest, stress-test, compare with benchmarks and retain a history of decisions.

### EXECUTE
Not an MK1 capability. A future deterministic capability only if separate gates close.

## Initial wedge

Spanish-first, mobile-first, beginner-to-intermediate self-directed investors interested in liquid crypto, ETFs and equities.

Spanish is a distribution wedge, not the moat.

## Moat hypothesis

1. Beginner-first financial translation.
2. Multi-source evidence model.
3. Decision Records and provenance.
4. Risk-first scenario framing.
5. Progressive disclosure: Beginner → Investor → Trader → Quant → Developer.
6. Explicit uncertainty and scientific validation.
7. Trust history: losing/failed analyses are not erased.
8. Deterministic user-defined automation only in later phases.

“AI + natural language + automation” is commodity-level differentiation and is explicitly insufficient.
