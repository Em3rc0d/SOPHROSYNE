# Product Thesis

## Category

**Financial Decision Intelligence**

Not an AI trading guru, not an autonomous hedge fund, not a signal feed.

## Problem

Some self-directed investors face fragmented information, poorly represented uncertainty and a tendency to confuse good outcomes with good decision processes.

The opportunity is not “more indicators”. It is to test whether **structured evidence + explicit uncertainty + disciplined feedback** can improve decision quality enough to justify a product.

## Primary JTBD

> Help me understand what is known, what is uncertain, what evidence supports or contradicts each plausible scenario, what would invalidate my view, and how to evaluate the quality of my decision process independently from luck.

## Core promise

> SOPHROSYNE does not promise better returns or certainty. It promises a system for making the reasoning behind financial decisions explicit, point-in-time, auditable and testable.

Any stronger claim requires evidence receipts.

## Thesis architecture

The project is governed by `docs/product/THESIS_STACK.md`.

The stack separates:
- T0 methodological invariant;
- T1 problem thesis;
- T2 causal intervention thesis;
- T3 product thesis;
- T4 economic thesis;
- T5 defensibility thesis.

Passing one layer never proves the next.

## Product modes

### EXPLAIN
Translate structured market state/evidence into plain language without inventing certainty.

### STRATEGIZE
Turn user-authored ideas into explicit, testable rules.

### VERIFY
Backtest, stress-test, compare with baselines and preserve point-in-time decision history.

### REFLECT
Compare expectations, confidence, evidence and later outcomes to evaluate decision-process quality without equating luck with skill.

### EXECUTE
Not an MK1 capability. A future deterministic capability only if separate gates close.

## Initial wedge

Candidate: Spanish-first, mobile-first, beginner-to-intermediate self-directed investors interested in liquid crypto, ETFs and equities.

This is a hypothesis, not a moat.

Spanish is distribution/localization only unless evidence proves a stronger locally specific advantage.

## Explicitly commodity / non-moat by itself

- translation;
- LLM chat;
- persistent memory;
- multi-source ingestion;
- backtesting;
- natural-language strategy building;
- Decision Records;
- progressive disclosure;
- generic automation.

These may be useful capabilities. They are not allowed to count as defensibility without Q05 evidence.

## Causal-value gate

Before user enthusiasm, pricing intent or moat narratives can be interpreted as core product validation, SOPHROSYNE must pass or conditionally pass:

`docs/validation/Q00_CORE_CAUSAL_VALUE.md`

Q00 deliberately compares the candidate product against strong simple baselines, including a five-question friction checklist, stateless assistance, simple feedback and memory ablations.

If simple/cheap substitutes capture the meaningful effect, complexity must be removed or the configuration stopped.

## Minimum viable empirical claim

Until stronger receipts exist, SOPHROSYNE may claim only:

> **Structured point-in-time feedback may help some users evaluate decisions under uncertainty more rigorously than an equivalent baseline.**

No claim of improved returns, superior investing skill, durable alpha, cognitive transformation or defensibility is implied.

## Decision-quality principles

A useful intervention must avoid trivial optimization toward:
- permanent low confidence;
- excessive `NO CONCLUSION`;
- analysis paralysis;
- rubric gaming;
- hindsight/outcome bias;
- false precision from correlated observations.

Calibration, discrimination, decision utility and cognitive cost are separate dimensions.

## Defensibility hypothesis

If a moat eventually exists, it must be a **compound validated advantage**, potentially involving point-in-time evidence/provenance, process-vs-outcome labels, calibrated behavioral error taxonomy, validated intervention policy and accumulated trust/learning assets.

No single feature is pre-declared as moat.

## Kill rule

The independent-product configuration must stop or materially pivot if robust evidence shows that:
1. a simple-friction baseline captures most of the causal benefit;
2. a realistic free/cheap substitute is non-inferior on primary product outcomes;
3. meaningful transfer beyond assisted use does not exist where such a claim is made;
4. willingness-to-pay cannot support the promotable cost structure; and
5. no durable accumulation advantage emerges.

Implementation quality and sunk cost cannot override this rule.

## Final product invariant

> **SOPHROSYNE earns complexity one validated layer at a time. Evidence is allowed to simplify or kill the product.**
