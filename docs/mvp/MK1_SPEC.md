# MK1 — Evidence MVP

## Goal

Prove that users understand markets faster and more accurately with evidence-based translation than with raw chart complexity, without inflating their confidence.

## In scope

### Market Translator
For a deliberately small asset set:
- market state;
- trend, momentum and volatility;
- relevant liquidity/context;
- supporting and contradicting evidence;
- uncertainty;
- invalidators.

### Evidence View
Progressive disclosure:
- Beginner — plain language;
- Investor — important factors;
- Trader — features/indicators;
- Quant — validation, raw metrics and provenance.

### Decision Ledger
Immutable historical Decision Records.

### Portfolio Context
Initially manual/read-only:
- allocation;
- concentration;
- overlap/correlation;
- volatility/drawdown context.

### Strategy Sandbox
User-authored simple deterministic rules → formalize → backtest → costs → benchmark → paper validation.

### Learning Layer
Explain why evidence matters and which evidence contradicts the interpretation.

## Out of scope

- custody;
- live trading;
- copy trading;
- strategy marketplace;
- autonomous agents;
- personalized security recommendations;
- leverage/options/futures;
- exact-price prediction;
- full charting platform;
- social feed.

## Initial asset scope

Start with BTC/USDT for rich 24/7 research/paper workflows and one or two broad US ETFs for portfolio/market-state translation, subject to appropriate data rights.

## Success metrics

- comprehension vs chart-first baseline;
- time-to-understanding;
- uncertainty calibration;
- repeat usage;
- evidence/invalidation expansion rate;
- paid intent/conversion;
- false-causality audit rate.
